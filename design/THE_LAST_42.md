## Two things to know before choosing

**The reef has no reef.** There is not one coral in the roster. Corals are animals, and
they are the thing the biome is named after and built from. Whatever else gets cut, the
reef-builders should not — that is the largest single gap anywhere in the dex.

**Farmland gets nothing from any batch, and it will end up thinnest.** It sits at 37 and
appears in none of the six lists above. Once the reef and the ocean fill, farmland is the
smallest biome in the game. The **mole** has also been outstanding since 2026-08-25 and was
never actioned.

## What that leaves out, and why

Nothing here is bad. These are the 42 losing to a cap, not to quality.

**Open ocean, 18 cut.** The fishery mostly goes — Yellowfin, Skipjack, Mahi-mahi, Wahoo —
because **Bluefin Tuna already carries the quota story** and the rest repeat it. So do
**Common Dolphin, Striped Dolphin** *(the roster holds eight dolphins)*, **Porbeagle**
*(ten sharks already)*, **Pilot Fish** *(the Remora does hitchhiking better)*, **Loggerhead
Turtle** *(five sea turtles already)*, and **Sargassum Crab** *(the Sargassum Fish carries
the Sargasso)*. Sooty Tern, Manx Shearwater, Storm Petrel, Blue Shark, Devil Ray, Remora,
Sargassum Fish and Olive Ridley are the strongest of the cut and the first to bring back.

**Coral reef, 8 cut.** Tube Sponge *(the Giant Barrel Sponge is already in)*, Cleaner Shrimp
*(the cleaner wrasse runs that station)*, Harlequin Shrimp, Spanish Dancer, Feather Star,
Garden Eel, Flamboyant Cuttlefish, Humphead Wrasse.

**Coast, 6 cut.** Sanderling *(four shorebirds already)*, Grey Seal and Harbour Seal, Sea
Hare, Goose Barnacle, Cormorant.

**Desert 3, mountains 4, deep sea 5 cut.** Deep sea is the fattest non-tropical biome at 55
and mountains sits at 48; both can wait.

## The alternative, if this is too tight

**Interest governs, not headcount** — that rule is already written down, and it cuts both
ways. If a batch is worth more than the round number, the round number is the thing that
should move, and that is Ayr's call rather than a tool's.

Three ways out, in the order I would suggest them:

1. **Take the 42 as proposed.** The confirmed batches lose about half, and what is left is
   the strange half rather than the redundant half.
2. **Let the surplus start the next tranche.** The 42 are cut as above, and the ~42 left
   over become the beginning of whatever comes after the 700 rather than being discarded.
   Nothing is lost, and `design/cut_species.md` does not grow.
3. **Move the 700.** 700 → 750 absorbs everything with room to spare. Ayr has said the
   round numbers are firm, so this is recorded rather than recommended.

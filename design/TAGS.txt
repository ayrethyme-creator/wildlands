! EW - EXTINCT IN THE WILD
! ============================================================================
! Why the tag exists at all. The "Only In Captivity" badge was written as a RULE -
! "any five from On the Brink tagged EW" - against a tag that had never been
! created. It was patched by typing the ten species into BADGES.txt by hand, which
! worked but left the badge as a list that nobody could check against anything.
!
! With this file the badge becomes a rule again, and Uncle Albert enforces three
! things that a hand-typed list could not:
!
!   1. every EW species is real and in the roster
!   2. every EW species sits in the "brink" group - because On the Brink admits on
!      status alone, Critically Endangered OR Extinct in the Wild, and EW is half
!      of that admission test
!   3. the "Only In Captivity" membership is EXACTLY this set - so adding a species
!      here without updating the badge, or the reverse, is a failure rather than a
!      quiet drift
!
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! FORMALLY LISTED EXTINCT IN THE WILD - seven
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Spix's Macaw          Cyanopsitta spixii. The one everybody knows from the film.
! Guam Kingfisher       Todiramphus cinnamominus. Wiped out by the brown tree snake;
!                         every living bird descends from 29 taken into captivity.
! Socorro Dove          Zenaida graysoni. Gone from Socorro by 1972; survives in
!                         European and American collections.
! 'Alala               Corvus hawaiiensis, the Hawaiian crow. Tool-using, and the
!                         releases keep failing to the 'io, the Hawaiian hawk.
! Kihansi Spray Toad    Nectophrynoides asperginis. Its entire world was the spray
!                         zone of one waterfall, and a dam took the spray away.
! Wyoming Toad          Anaxyrus baxteri.
! Pere David's Deer     Elaphurus davidianus. Extinct in China, survived because a
!                         herd sat in an English park at Woburn. Reintroduced herds
!                         exist and the listing has not moved.
!
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! EXTINCT IN THE WILD AS A POPULATION, NOT AS AN IUCN SPECIES LISTING - three
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! These three carry a caveat and the field-guide entry should say so plainly. They
! are tagged because the badge's CONCEPT - nowhere left to go home to - is exactly
! true of them, and dropping them would teach that the line is cleaner than it is.
!
! Barbary Lion          A population of Panther leo, not a species. Gone from the
!                         wild; survives only as captive descendants. The lion as a
!                         species is Vulnerable.
! Northern White Rhino  The subspecies Ceratotherium simum cottoni. TWO FEMALES,
!                         both unable to carry a calf. Functionally rather than
!                         formally EW, and the starkest entry in the game.
! South China Tiger     Panthera tigris amoyensis. Listed Critically Endangered with
!                         no confirmed wild animal for over thirty years.
!
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! DELIBERATELY NOT TAGGED - read this before adding one
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Przewalski's Horse    WAS EW and has been reintroduced. It belongs in "Back From
!                         the Brink", where it already is, and the contrast with
!                         this list is worth using rather than blurring.
! Scimitar-horned Oryx  Same story, same reason.
! Guam Rail             Declared EW, rebred, released on Cocos Island, and downlisted
!                         to Critically Endangered in 2019 - the second bird ever to
!                         recover from EW. It is in "Grounded" and "Back From the
!                         Brink" instead.
! Arabian Oryx          The first species ever downlisted from EW, in 2011. Now
!                         Vulnerable, which is why it is not in On the Brink at all.
! Axolotl               Often called extinct in the wild in ordinary speech, and it
!                         is not - it is Critically Endangered, with a small wild
!                         population left in Xochimilco. The GDD's phrase is
!                         "functionally extinct in the wild", which is the honest one.
!
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! UNRESOLVED - Partula Snail. FLAGGED FOR AYR, 2026-08-29.
! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Found by Uncle Albert check 11 the moment the game's own status field became
! readable: THE GAME DATA CALLS IT EW AND THIS FILE DOES NOT TAG IT. Three separate
! problems are stacked in one entry, which is why it is written down rather than
! decided quietly.
!
!   1. THE NAME IS A GENUS, NOT A SPECIES. Partula holds something over a hundred
!      described species. It is the same fault the generics audit spent a week
!      removing, and it slipped through because Albert's bare-name check looks for
!      the single word "snail" and this reads as specific.
!   2. THE GENUS SPANS THREE STATUSES AT ONCE. Introduced rosy wolfsnails wiped out
!      Partula across Pacific islands. Some species are extinct outright, some
!      survive only in zoo populations, and some have been reintroduced to Tahiti
!      and Moorea from those populations. "Partula Snail" is EX, EW and CR
!      simultaneously depending which species is meant - so NO single tag is right
!      while the name stays generic.
!   3. CORRECTED 2026-08-29: an earlier version of this note said it sits in The
!      Vigil and is therefore in the wrong group. THAT WAS WRONG. GROUND_TRUTH.txt
!      has it in vigil, but PENDING_MOVES.txt already moves it to "brink", and On
!      the Brink admits Critically Endangered OR Extinct in the Wild - so EW is
!      exactly right for it there and there is no group conflict at all. The claim
!      was made by reading the ground truth without applying the pipeline on top,
!      which is the one thing every tool in here exists to stop a person doing.
!      (The GAME's entry text still calls it "the clearest entry in the Vigil", so
!      that prose is now stale and needs a pass when the species is named.)
!
! It is not tagged EW here, because tagging a genus would be asserting something
! this file exists to prevent. THE FIX IS TO NAME THE SPECIES, and the tag follows
! from whichever one is chosen. Ayr's call.
!
! IUCN listings move. When one does, change it here and Albert will tell you which
! badge stopped being true.
! ============================================================================

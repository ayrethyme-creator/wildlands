# New species to create

Running list for the new game. **Interest governs, not headcount** — the ~57
floor was a working guide and is explicitly not a target. A biome gets what is
worth having in it.

Status: **CONFIRMED** = Ayr has approved it. **LIKELY** = approved in principle,
not finally.

---

## DEEP SEA — CONFIRMED, 32 species

*Ayr, 2026-08-25: "all of those should be included definitely."*
Deep sea currently holds 10 moved out of open ocean, so this takes it to **42**.

### Things that should not be possible

| Species | Why |
|---|---|
| **Giant tube worm** | Two metres tall at hydrothermal vents, with **no mouth, no gut and no anus**. Farms bacteria inside its own body that live on vent chemicals |
| **Yeti crab** | Grows bacteria on the hair of its arms, **waves them over the vent to feed them**, then eats them |
| **Barreleye** | A **transparent head** with tubular eyes inside it, looking up through its own skull |
| **Bone-eating snot flower worm** *(Osedax)* | Lives only on whale skeletons, bores in with acid. The males are microscopic and live **inside the female**, by the dozen |
| **Blobfish** | Only ugly because the famous photograph is **decompression injury**. At depth it looks like an ordinary fish |

### Record holders

| Species | Why |
|---|---|
| **Siphonophore** *(Apolemia)* | A colony behaving as one animal. Longest recorded: **45 m**, longer than a blue whale |
| **Snailfish** | **The deepest fish ever recorded**, over 8 km down in the Mariana Trench |
| **Bristlemouth** | Almost certainly **the most numerous vertebrate on Earth**, and virtually unknown |
| **Fangtooth** | The largest teeth of any fish relative to its size; it cannot fully close its mouth |
| **Cuvier's beaked whale** | **Deepest dive of any mammal** — nearly 3 km, over three hours on one breath. *(Currently in open ocean; moving it here drops that biome to 22)* |

### Light in the dark

| Species | Why |
|---|---|
| **Atolla jellyfish** | Fires a **spinning burst of blue light** when attacked — a burglar alarm, meant to draw something bigger to eat its attacker |
| **Stoplight loosejaw** | Shines **red light**, which almost nothing down there can see. It hunts with an invisible torch |
| **Hatchetfish** | Lights its own underside to erase its silhouette against the faint light above |
| **Lanternfish** | Rises to the surface nightly and sinks by dawn — **the largest animal migration on the planet, twice a day** |
| **Vampire squid** | Neither. Eats **marine snow**, the drifting remains of everything that died higher up |

### The rest

Dumbo octopus · Gulper eel · Viperfish · **Black swallower** *(swallows prey ten
times its own mass)* · **Cookiecutter shark** *(takes round plugs out of living
whales)* · Megamouth shark · Sixgill shark · Ghost shark / chimaera · Sea pig ·
Giant isopod · Tripod fish · Glass squid · **Bigfin squid** *(arms trailing
seven metres behind it)* · **Venus flower basket** *(a glass sponge that traps a
pair of shrimp inside for life — given as wedding gifts in Japan)* · Deep-sea
dragonfish · Sea toad · Deep-sea cucumber

### Quests that arrive with this biome

- **Deep-sea mining** — vent communities and manganese nodule fields. The live
  conservation fight of this decade, and almost unknown.
- **Whale fall** — an entire ecosystem that exists only because something huge
  died, lasting decades.
- **Bottom trawling** — flattening seafloor communities that took centuries.

## POLAR — LIKELY, 25 species

*Ayr, 2026-08-25: "those are all great. we will most likely do that."*
Takes polar from 28 to **53**.

**Extraordinary:** Antarctic icefish *(the only vertebrate with no haemoglobin —
colourless blood)* · Colossal squid *(largest eyes of any animal that has ever
lived)* · Collared lemming *(grows an extra pair of digging claws each autumn
and sheds them in spring)* · Antarctic sea spider *(polar gigantism — dinner
plate sized)* · Bearded seal *(the males sing a descending spiral audible for
miles under ice)* · Hooded seal *(inflates a red balloon out of one nostril)*

**Records:** Arctic tern *(longest migration of any animal — two summers a
year)* · Snow petrel *(nests 300 km inland on bare Antarctic rock)* · South
polar skua · Long-tailed duck · Little auk

**Penguins:** Adélie *(steals stones from neighbours)* · King *(fourteen-month
chicks)* · Gentoo *(fastest underwater)* · Chinstrap

**Fish:** Antarctic toothfish *(the Ross Sea MPA fight)* · Arctic cod *(lives
inside brine channels in the ice)* · Capelin *(the fish everything eats)* ·
Arctic char · Greenland halibut

**Others:** Ringed seal *(builds snow caves; the polar bear's actual prey)* ·
Arctic wolf · Rock ptarmigan · Ivory gull *(follows polar bears; declining and
nobody knows why)* · Ross's gull

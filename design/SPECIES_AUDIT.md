# SPECIES AUDIT — errors in the current roster

Prompted by Ayr, 2026-08-26: *"the fire hawk in savanna is actually 3 different species.
Check all of the animals we currently have for errors like that and similar."*

Ayr is right, and it is not an isolated case. Roughly **a quarter of the roster is not a
species** — it is a genus, a family, a sex, a caste, or an invention. In a game whose
whole claim is that every entry can be credited to something real, that matters.

**This covers the clear cases.** It is not a species-by-species taxonomic verification of
all 528 animals; it is every error I can identify with confidence.

---

## 1. NOT AN ANIMAL AT ALL — 4 entries  ✅ ALL FOUR APPLIED 2026-08-26

| Entry | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Firehawk** | Ayr's example. Not a species. **Three unrelated Australian raptors** are documented carrying burning sticks to spread fire: **black kite, whistling kite, brown falcon** | Pick one. **Black kite** is the best documented and the most widespread |
| **Lioness** | A **sex**, not a species | Merge with Maned Lion |
| **Maned Lion** | Also a sex. **These two entries are one animal** | One entry: **Lion**. Male and female become an appearance state, like the House Cat coats |
| **Mole-Rat Queen** | A **caste**, not a species. Naked Mole-Rat already exists as a life-stage entry | One entry: **Naked Mole-Rat**. The queen is a life stage of it — and a genuinely extraordinary one, since she grows extra vertebrae when she becomes queen |

## 4. BIOLOGICALLY WRONG PLACEMENT

| Animal | Currently | Should be | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ring-tailed Lemur** | Desert | **Rainforest** or forest | Madagascan gallery forest and spiny scrub. Not a desert animal |
| **Sifaka** | Desert | **Rainforest** or forest | Madagascan forest |
| **Komodo Dragon** | Rainforest | **Savanna** | It lives in dry savanna woodland and grassland on Indonesian islands, not rainforest |
| **Coelacanth** | Open ocean | **Deep sea** | Lives at 150–250 m in submarine caves. Also the deep sea badly needs species |
| **Agouti** | Savanna | **Rainforest** | Neotropical forest floor |
| **Pallas's Cat** | Savanna | **Mountains** | Central Asian steppe and rocky high country |
| **Patagonian Mara** | Mountains | **Savanna** | Patagonian steppe — open plain, not mountain |

**Defensible but worth a look:** Serval and Jaguar in wetland; American Bison, Prairie
Dog, Pronghorn and Swift Fox in savanna, which are North American prairie animals sharing
a biome with African savanna.

## The scale of it

- **4** entries that are not animals
- **11** redundant pairs
- **~85** entries that name a group rather than a species
- **7** clear placement errors

**Roughly 100 of the 528 need a decision.** None of them is hard — in nearly every case
there is one obvious species that carries the interesting fact — but it is a real pass,
and it should happen **before** the 122 new animals are written, so the new ones are
named properly from the start.

Species Audit

About 110 of the 611 real animals in the roster are not a species — they are a genus, a family, a sex, a caste, or an invention. In a game whose claim is that every entry can be credited to something real, each one needs a decision.

"The fire hawk in savanna is actually 3 different species. Check all of the animals we currently have for errors like that and similar." — Ayr, 26 August

Not an animal at all — 4

These do not name a creature. They name a behaviour, a sex, or a job inside a colony.

Firehawksavanna
Ayr's example. Not a species — three unrelated Australian raptors are documented carrying burning sticks to spread fire: black kite, whistling kite and brown falcon. Black kite — best documented and most widespread
Lionesssavanna
A sex, not a species. Merge with Maned Lion
Maned Lionsavanna
Also a sex. These two entries are one animal. One entry: Lion. Male and female become an appearance state, like the House Cat coats
Mole-Rat Queensavanna
A caste, not a species — and Naked Mole-Rat already exists as a life-stage entry. One entry: Naked Mole-Rat. The queen becomes a life stage of it — and a remarkable one, since she grows extra vertebrae on becoming queen

Redundant pairs — 11

The general and the specific of the same animal, both present. A player reads these as one creature listed twice.

Gibbon + Siamang
A siamang is a gibbon. Name the other one — lar gibbon
Dragonet + Mandarinfish
A mandarinfish is a dragonet. Straight duplicate. Keep Mandarinfish
Jackal + Black-backed Jackal
"Jackal" covers three species. Keep the specific one
Fur Seal + Cape Fur Seal
Nine species of fur seal. Keep Cape fur seal
Lynx + Iberian Lynx
Four species of lynx. Name the general one — Eurasian lynx
Fruit Bat + Egyptian Fruit Bat + Spectacled Flying Fox
Three entries, one of which is a category containing the other two. Drop the generic
Otter + Giant Otter + Sea Otter
Thirteen species of otter. Name the generic — Eurasian otter

This covers every error identifiable with confidence. It is not a species-by-species taxonomic verification of all 611 animals. The pass should run before the last 39 new species are written, or those get made to the same standard and it has to happen twice.
